Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity? +

Most products start at just 1,000 units (some accessories at 2,000). We aggregate orders from multiple restaurants, so you get bulk factory pricing without a bulk commitment.

How does the AI packaging design work? +

Upload your logo and brand colours, and our AI generates 4 professional packaging designs in under 5 minutes. Refine them online until you're happy — no designer needed, free of charge.

How long do production and delivery take? +

Quote and design mockup within 24 hours. Production typically takes 2–3 weeks, followed by delivery anywhere in Canada — or phased batch delivery from our warehouse.

Are your products compliant with Canada's single-use plastics rules? +

Yes. All foodPac products use biodegradable or compostable materials (kraft paper, PLA, CPLA), fully compliant with Canada's Single-use Plastics Prohibition Regulations — at no price premium.

What if I don't have a logo yet? +

No problem. Tell us about your restaurant and our team will help with branding — from a simple logo to a full packaging identity — before generating your designs.

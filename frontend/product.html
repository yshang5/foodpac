FAQs

How can I place an order?+

Choose your size and quantity, attach a design (or create one with AI), then click "Start Custom Quote". Our team will contact you within 24 hours with a confirmed quote and print-ready artwork.

I don't have a design. Can you help?+

Yes — click "Custom Design" on this page and our AI creates branded mockups in minutes, free. Our team polishes the chosen design into print-ready artwork before production.

How long until I receive my order?+

Production typically takes 2–3 weeks after artwork approval, followed by delivery anywhere in Canada. Phased batch delivery from our warehouse is available.

Is this product eco-friendly?+

Yes. All foodPac products use biodegradable or compostable materials, fully compliant with Canada's Single-use Plastics Prohibition Regulations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before ordering custom packaging.

What is the minimum order quantity? +

Most products start at just 1,000 units (some accessories like lids and sleeves start at 2,000). We aggregate orders from multiple restaurants, so you get bulk factory pricing without a bulk commitment.

How does the AI packaging design work? +

Upload your logo and brand colours, and our AI generates multiple professional packaging design options in under 5 minutes. You can refine them online until you're happy — no designer needed, and the design service is free.

How long do production and delivery take? +

You'll receive a quote and design mockup within 24 hours. Production typically takes 2–3 weeks, followed by delivery anywhere in Canada. We can also warehouse your stock and deliver it in batches as you need it.

Do you deliver across Canada? +

Yes. We deliver to restaurants nationwide — Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal, Calgary, Ottawa, Edmonton and beyond. Phased batch delivery is available so you never pay to store months of stock.

Are your products compliant with Canada's single-use plastics rules? +

Yes. All foodPac products use biodegradable or compostable materials (kraft paper, PLA, CPLA) and are fully compliant with Canada's Single-use Plastics Prohibition Regulations — at no price premium over generic packaging.

What if I don't have a logo or brand assets yet? +

No problem. Tell us about your restaurant in the quote form and our team will help with branding — from a simple logo to a full packaging identity — before generating your designs.
